# Test in a second browser and on desktop

> **What to check**
>
> This is obvious but still a check you should do. Your app works in the browser you don't use. Safari and Chrome render things differently, especially forms, dates, and payments.

---

## How to check it

1. Repeat your core flow in **Safari** (if you develop in Chrome) or **Chrome** (if you use Safari)
2. Do it once on **desktop** and once on **mobile**
3. Watch for broken layouts, buttons that don't react, inputs that behave weirdly

---

## What bad looks like

Everything works in Chrome and the date picker is dead in Safari. Half your mobile visitors use Safari, because every iPhone does.

---

## Why it matters

You built and tested in one browser. Your users pick their own.
