# Do an end-to-end user journey test

> **What to check**
>
> The one path that matters: land on your site, sign up, reach the thing your app actually does, pay if you charge. Do it on your phone, in an incognito tab, logged out. That's how every stranger experiences your app on launch day.

---

## How to check it

1. Open your app in an **incognito window** on your **phone**
2. Create a **brand-new account** with a fresh email
3. Go all the way to the moment your app **delivers its value**
4. If you charge: complete a **real test payment**

---

## What bad looks like

A signup button that does nothing on mobile. A confirmation email that never arrives. A payment form that errors out on step 2.

---

## Why it matters

You test logged in, on your laptop, every day. Your users get the version you never test.
