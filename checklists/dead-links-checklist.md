# Check for dead links and broken buttons

> **What to check**
>
> No dead ends: every link goes somewhere real, every button does something.

---

## How to check it

### 1. Manual check
Open every page you have (there aren't that many, that's fine).

Click every link in your navigation, footer, and content.

### 2. AI prompt

```
Go through my app and list every internal link and button. Check each one leads to a real page or action. List everything that's broken, missing, or goes to a placeholder.
```

---

## What bad looks like

A "Terms" link that 404s. A footer "Twitter" icon pointing to `#`. A pricing button that goes to a page that doesn't exist yet.

---

## Why it matters

Visitors hit one dead link and assume the whole app is unfinished.
