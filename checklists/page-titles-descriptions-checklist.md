# Give every public page a real title and description

> **What to check**
>
> Each public page has its own title (what shows in the browser tab and on Google) and a one-sentence description. Not "Create Next App". Not the same title on every page.

---

## How to check it

### 1. Manual check
Open each public page and look at the browser tab.

### 2. AI prompt

```
Check every public page of my app for: a unique title tag (50-60 characters, most important words first) and a unique meta description (140-155 characters, what the page offers + soft call to action). Fix what's missing. Write them in plain language, no keyword stuffing.
```

---

## What bad looks like

The browser tab says "Create Next App", the default nobody changed. Every visitor and Google both see it.

---

## Why it matters

The title is your headline on Google. People read it before they decide to click.
