# Score 9+ on mail-tester

> **What to check**
>
> An objective score for your email setup, catching whatever the other checks missed.

---

## How to check it

1. Go to [mail-tester.com](https://www.mail-tester.com). It gives you a one-time email address.
2. Trigger your app to send an email there, for example by signing up with that address.
3. Click "check your score". You want **9/10 or better**.

---

## What bad looks like

A **5/10** with a list of reasons: missing DMARC, a blacklisted IP, broken links, image-only content.

---

## Why it matters

It's a free 2-minute second opinion on your whole email setup, and it lists exactly what to fix.
