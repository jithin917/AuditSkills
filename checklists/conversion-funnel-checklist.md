# Set up a conversion funnel

> **What to check**
>
> At least one funnel that answers: of the people who land on my page, how many sign up, and where do the rest give up?

---

## How to check it

1. In **PostHog** (or your analytics tool), create a funnel with 3 to 4 steps:

    `Visited landing page → Clicked sign up → Created account → Reached the core feature`

2. That's it. One funnel. You can add more later.

---

## What bad looks like

"I got 500 visitors and 3 signups" with zero idea where the other 497 left. Was it the headline? The signup form? A broken step? Without a funnel, you're guessing.

---

## Why it matters

A funnel turns "launch went meh" into "84% dropped at the signup form." Then you know what to fix.
