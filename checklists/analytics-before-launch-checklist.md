# Set up analytics before launch day

> **What to check**
>
> On launch day you'll want to know: where do visitors come from, and what do they do. If analytics isn't running before launch, that valuable data is gone. There's no way to measure yesterday's visitors…

---

## How to check it

### 1. Choose your analytics tool
If you don't have analytics yet: **PostHog** is free at your scale (the free tier is insanely generous), it's open source, and its install wizard basically sets itself up (honestly they are great!).

### 2. Verify it's actually firing
Installed is not the same as working.

Open your live site in an **incognito tab**, then check your analytics dashboard's **real-time view**. Do you see yourself?

### 3. Check it works on the live domain, not just localhost

---

## What bad looks like

Launch day brings 2,000 visitors. Analytics shows 0, because the snippet only ran in development.

---

## Why it matters

Your launch traffic only happens once. Without analytics you'll never know which post drove it or why nobody signed up.
